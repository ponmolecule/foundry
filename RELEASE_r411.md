# r411 — Separate income balance basis for cash allocations

Cash allocations can now select Income calculation → Yield on a separate balance using the existing typed balance-source and annual-yield editors. The separate money basis is used once for each period's income calculation and never adds an asset or changes the allocation formula. It is not averaged again by Interest measured on; that control continues to measure the allocated stock for ordinary yield and any cost. The current editor keeps the new controls together under Income & cost and widens the method selector so the choice is readable.

This supports externally specified lagged/adjusted income bases without pasting interest amounts. The income basis may be an entered schedule or supported existing linked source. For a quarterly-only source, enter the quarter's average beginning-balance basis as a quarterly step series; monthly income uses that basis times the annual yield / 12. Changing yield or the basis recalculates income. This release does not automatically infer lagged bases from the component's own balance history: externally entered bases must be updated when source balances change.

Cash remains part of the existing bank cash balance and must fit available funding. Capped cash allocations cannot use an independent income basis, because the cap could reduce the booked balance independently; invalid combinations are rejected. Remaining bank cash retains its existing yield method. Entered income is not enabled for cash. Existing engagements retain default behavior; no automatic migrations and no save changes.
