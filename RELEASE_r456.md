# r456 — Unused commitment reserves and calculation freshness

Adds an opt-in unused_commitment_reserve contract to amortized-cost Profile A lending. The funded loan remains the balance owner. Monthly utilization and lifetime loss-rate Series support Flat / Growth / Schedule. Utilization must be greater than zero and no more than 100%; loss rate is a share in [0,100%]. Every monthly observation is validated; quarterly targets sample quarter-end rates. An explicit opening reserve supports existing exposures and provision releases. Profile B, fair-value and originate-to-sell combinations fail closed.

Total commitment = funded ending principal / utilization. Unused commitment = total less funded. Reserve liability = unused commitment × lifetime loss rate (not annualized). Provision is the change in reserve, with releases allowed; no charge-offs arise directly on undrawn commitments. The existing scenario reserve multiplier also applies to the unused target. The reserve posts to Other liabilities, independently of funded ALLL; its provision joins income-statement credit provision, taxes and retained earnings. Existing Call Report Other liabilities mapping consumes the combined liability. This feature does not add undrawn-exposure regulatory CCF/RWA modeling.

Product results and Numbers expose total commitment, unused commitment, the reserve and its provision. Reporting products include the extra provision in the provision memo subtotal and reconcile the separate liability against bank results. Direct contribution retains its existing charge-off-based definition; it is not provision-based net profit. Calculation audit gains an Unused Commitments sheet. No client data or default financial assumptions are hard-coded. Existing configurations remain off unless explicitly enabled; deployment performs no migration or automatic opt-in.

A visible calculation badge distinguishes Calculating, Up to date, Failed and results not current. Accepted runs are tied to the complete submitted configuration. Late responses cannot replace newer inputs. Numbers windows remain explicit snapshots; an older snapshot is marked and copying is disabled until it is reopened after a successful current run. Frozen snapshots remain inspectable. Pricing Numbers now also includes fee income.

Validation: five new reserve tests cover ledger build/release/opening, no unused balance at full utilization, invalid shares and unsupported profile, monthly/quarterly consistency and intra-quarter validation, full statement postings, funded-ledger isolation, reporting reconciliation, audit export and saved JSON replay. Four r453 live population tests and two existing reporting identity/export tests passed. Chromium tests cover real product-card controls, precise inputs/persistence, result rows, pending/current/failed status, stale snapshot copy blocking, late response rejection and recovery; prior r455 shared-deletion and r452 Apply/headline regressions pass. All six pinned complete-run/financial fingerprints remain unchanged. The confidential client engagement itself is not available for testing.
