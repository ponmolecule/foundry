# Other income entry

1. Open Configuration → Other income.
2. Click + Add income line.
3. Enter the engagement-specific name; choose Dividend income when appropriate.
4. Source: Enter. Select Explicit for the Income amount.
5. Amount period: Month; Schedule cadence: Month for monthly actual income amounts.
6. Paste the full monthly net-income schedule in $000s; click Load (replace). Exclude annual summary columns.
7. Run and inspect Income Statement → Other income. In the audit workbook use Other Income for named detail; Income Statement → otherIncome for the total, and All Series for published series.

Enter only income not already posted elsewhere. The parent entity's underlying income and expense components are provenance, not additional bank entries.
