# Foundry r381 — minimum deposit interest expense and compact pricing controls

Built on r380 / e47b3f8 and the subsequent expense-accrual layout and backlog commits. Packaged; not deployed.

Deposit Pricing now offers an optional Minimum interest expense amount in $000s, with Month / Quarter / Year amount period and the existing Flat / Growth / Explicit schedule editor. Each model period posts the greater of balance-based interest and the converted monetary minimum, once as deposit interest expense. Percentage rate floors/caps remain separate. The rule works with fixed and floating pricing in both profiles, including Profile A level, customer-linked and pool methods. Existing Profile B balance-method restrictions remain unchanged. No saved engagement is opted in or assigned a minimum automatically.

The floor applies once per model period: 30 ($000s)/month means a 90 ($000s) quarterly-equivalent minimum in a quarterly model, not a promise to sum three individually floored monthly accruals. Use monthly cadence to reproduce a monthly payment floor. Negative/nonfinite/incomplete inputs and unsupported source/period contracts fail validation. Calculation Audit and unrounded diagnostics expose calculated interest, the minimum and the resulting top-up.

Deposit pricing controls now follow a stable order: rate type/index, beta/spread, percentage floor/cap, interest balance measure, optional monetary minimum, retained-balance fees. Field widths align in the existing grid. Short segment captions retain descriptive tooltips. The duplicate rate explanation is removed; the left equation rail includes the minimum when enabled. Live result panels and collapse/expand remain in place. The expense-accrual dropdown is constrained to a compact 320px control, aligned within the formula card, with help text beside it that wraps on narrow screens.
